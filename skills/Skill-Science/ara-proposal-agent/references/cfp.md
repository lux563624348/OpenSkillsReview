# Amazon 2030 Call for Proposals

Official source:

- https://www.amazon.science/research-awards/call-for-proposals/amazon-2030-call-for-proposals-spring-2026

## Use this CFP for

- Amazon Research Awards proposals for the Spring 2026 cycle
- Research framed around scale-driven innovation and real-world impact in the next three to five years
- Proposal drafting, positioning, and compliance checks

## Practical takeaways

- Keep the pitch grounded in scientific rigor and technical feasibility.
- Emphasize why scale matters to the research problem.
- Frame the work as more than an incremental improvement.
- Use the official proposal template when writing the submission.
